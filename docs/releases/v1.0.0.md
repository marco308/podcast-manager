The first public release of Podcast Manager, a self-hosted app that splits your Spotify podcast feed into playlists you design and rebuilds them every day.

**New here?** Start with the [project page](https://marco308.github.io/podcast-manager/) for what it does, and the [README](https://github.com/marco308/podcast-manager#setup) for how to run it.

## What's in 1.0

- **Managed playlists.** Assign podcasts to playlists, and the app creates and rebuilds the matching Spotify playlist. It can also take over a Spotify playlist you already own.
- **Per-show rules.** Each show in each playlist gives all unplayed episodes, only the latest, or up to *n*, taken from the newest or oldest end.
- **Serials in order.** Mark a story podcast as sequential and it gives its next unheard episode instead of its newest.
- **Two arrangements.** Shows in the drag-and-drop order you set, or everything merged by release date.
- **Daily upkeep.** Every day the app syncs your Spotify library and rebuilds your enabled playlists. Played episodes are removed every 30 minutes.
- **Library housekeeping.** Archive shows you don't want to see. Shows you unfollow stop contributing at once and are removed after 7 days.
- **iOS companion app** in `ios/`, built from source.

## Before you install

- **Spotify Premium and your own developer app.** Since March 2026, Spotify's Development Mode needs Premium on the account that owns the app, allows one Dev Mode app per developer, and caps it at 5 allowlisted users.
- **One Spotify account per installation.** Registration closes after the first sign-in.
- **HTTPS.** Spotify won't sign you in over plain HTTP.

## Upgrading an existing installation

- **The weekend-only setting is gone** (#238). Its column is dropped by migration `018`. The API still sends `is_weekend_only: false`, for older iOS builds.
- **Linking existing playlists needs a new permission** (`playlist-read-private`, #245). Sign out and back in once to grant it.
- **Migrations run on container start.** `alembic upgrade head` runs automatically.
