A feature and fixes release: a shuffled arrangement, serials that play in the right order when several parts drop on the same day, and in-app account deletion.

## Highlights

- **Shuffled playlists** (#278). A third arrangement next to *In podcast order* and *By release date*: podcasts are mixed in a fresh random order on every rebuild, while each podcast's own episodes still play in order, so a serial never plays out of sequence.
- **Same-day serial episodes in release order** (#275). When a sequential show releases several parts on one day, they now play part 1 first. Before, the build could pick part 2 and skip part 1.
- **Editing a playlist opens that playlist** (#276). The edit form could open filled in with the playlist you edited before. Saving it would have copied that playlist's name, Spotify link and settings onto this one.
- **Delete your account** (#271). Settings in the web app and on iOS can delete your account and everything stored for it. Your Spotify playlists are left as they are, and registration reopens for the next sign-in.
- **The theme hint matches your choice** (#262). Settings described the theme as "System" whichever option you picked.
- **iOS app** (#268, #269, #273, #274): a **Try the demo** mode that runs on sample data without a server, App Store readiness fixes (privacy manifest, display name, a privacy policy page), and iPhone-only for this version.

## Upgrading

- **Nothing to do.** No database migrations and no configuration changes since 1.0: pull the new images and restart.
- **Versioned images.** From this release on, the images CI publishes are also tagged with the version (`:1.1.0`, plus `:1.1` and `:1`), not only the commit, so `deploy.sh` can pin a release with `IMAGE_TAG=1.1.0`.
